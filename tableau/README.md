# Tableau Dashboards

This directory contains the Tableau business intelligence
deliverables developed for the MIS581 capstone.

The workbook includes six analytical worksheets and two
interactive dashboards:

## Historical Market Dynamics
- Annual housing-price changes
- Metropolitan unemployment trends
- Inflation-adjusted household income

## Metropolitan Market Overview
- Metropolitan population map
- Residential building permits per 1,000 residents
- National 30-year fixed mortgage rates

The dashboards support historical regional comparisons
across seven U.S. metropolitan areas from 2005 to 2024.

Interactive features include metropolitan highlighting,
selected-year filtering, dynamic titles, and navigation
between dashboards.

The packaged Tableau workbook (.twbx) will be added after
the final publication and data-security review.

The visualizations are descriptive and should not be
interpreted as causal estimates or housing-price forecasts.
